Building the Future of Compliance & Automation

Jasmine R. Thomas is an entrepreneur and seasoned professional whose career spans a wide range of industries and disciplines. She is currently the CEO of OA AI, a startup dedicated to building intelligent automations for clients and optimizing workflows — saving organizations significant time and money by fully leveraging the power of AI and automated systems.

With well over ten years of experience, Jasmine has built a reputation for leading high‑performing teams and championing meaningful change. She holds the Project Management Professional (PMP) certification and the Certified Scrum Master (CSM) credential, giving her a unique ability to bridge strategic vision with agile execution.

Her expertise in ISO 27001 information security management and ISO 9001 quality management systems allows her to guide organizations through complex compliance landscapes — turning regulatory obligations into genuine competitive advantages.

Beyond compliance, Jasmine is passionate about the intersection of AI and business operations. She designs and builds automation tools, AI agent‑generated websites, and custom solutions that help mid‑market companies operate at the speed of technology.

Jasmine graduated with her Associate of Science in Biology from Merced College, a foundation that sharpened her analytical thinking and scientific approach to problem‑solving — skills she carries into every engagement.
